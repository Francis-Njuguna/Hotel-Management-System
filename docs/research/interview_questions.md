# Appendix B · Interview questions (blank guide)

**For the investigator:** use only after the activities and safeguards in the [investigation plan](investigation_plan.md) have been agreed. Confirm voluntary consent, private note handling and the option to stop. Do not request names, live records, photos, recordings or identifying anecdotes. Ask only about work the participant knows. There are no interview responses or claimed findings in this guide.

## Start with practice, not features

| Prompt | Optional neutral probe |
|---|---|
| I1. What starts a typical piece of work for you, and how do you know it is finished? | What information reaches you, who needs the result, and what varies? “Not part of my work” is a useful answer. |
| I2. How, if at all, do staff recognize a returning guest and decide which earlier information is relevant? | What if two people have the same name? Describe information types, not values. |
| I3. Talk through a room enquiry that becomes a booking. | What commits the room? What happens with several rooms, changed dates, cancellation or someone asking for the same nights? |
| I4. What distinguishes a room available for a future booking from one a guest may enter now? | How do arrival, actual departure, unfinished cleaning and repairs become known? Who decides if there is doubt? |
| I5. How does cleaning or repair work get assigned, completed and checked? | What if a worker becomes unavailable or work is reported finished but the room is still unusable? Who can release it? |
| I6. If a meal is requested, how is it accepted, fulfilled, changed or cancelled? | How are unavailable items handled? If it is for a room rather than dine-in, how is the proper bill chosen? |
| I7. How does a charge become part of a bill? Separately, how is money recognized as received? | What distinguishes a tender awaiting confirmation, a confirmed receipt and a repeated entry? Who may reconcile them? No real references or amounts. |
| I8. How is it decided whether a guest can depart with work or money outstanding? | Ask about pending food orders, early departure, partial payment and who can authorize an exception, **if** these situations are relevant. |
| I9. How is parking assigned or released, if applicable? | What happens when two requests compete for the same space? What vehicle information is needed, and who may see it? |
| I10. Who needs which tasks, histories and totals during a shift, and who should not see them? | What changes when a worker joins, changes duty or leaves? For one metric, ask what event counts, when its day begins/ends, and how to check it. |

## Explore a possible rule separately

Choose a relevant **invented** scenario below, only after hearing the participant's account. Ask: “What should the application do? Who should decide? What should it preserve or refuse?” Do not imply that the build default is the hotel's current or preferred rule.

- Two staff request overlapping nights for one named room; one request also includes a second room (FR-03/04; QA-02–03).
- A room was cleaned, but inspection or a blocking repair is unfinished when the next guest arrives (FR-03/05; QA-17).
- A meal is ready but not served when checkout starts, or the same confirmed tender is entered twice (FR-06/07; QA-07, QA-09, QA-14).

**Close:** read back a short paraphrase. Ask what is inaccurate, which statements describe current work versus a desired change, and what remains unknown or should not be retained. Record uncertainty; silence is not agreement. Keep any permitted notes private. These prompts are not a substitute for actual evidence or hotel approval.
